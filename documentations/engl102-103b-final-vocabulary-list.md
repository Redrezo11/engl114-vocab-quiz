# ENGL 102 / 103B — Final Exam Vocabulary List

Source: *Final Exam Specs* PDF ("الاختبار النهائي المحوسب 40% — ENG 101 (Humanities)", S2 2024–2025), pages 2–3, "Vocabulary List".
Used for the separate **ENGL 102/103B Final Practice** course (`courses/engl102-final/`). The midterm list is in
[engl102-103b-vocabulary-list.md](engl102-103b-vocabulary-list.md) and is not changed by this file.

English headwords and parts of speech are copied from the source as written. The source labels *on purpose* and
*except for* as "Phrasal Verb"; that label is kept here even though they work as an adverb phrase and a preposition.
The **Arabic** column was written for the site's Word Bank, not taken from the course book, so review is welcome. The live data is in
[`courses/engl102-final/wordbank.json`](../courses/engl102-final/wordbank.json), which is generated from this table.

- **Total entries:** 164.
- **Overlap with the midterm list:** 21 words (avoid, behavior, control, dangerous, describe, difficulty, enjoyable,
  event, plan, positive, prepare, produce, provide, reduce, serious, simple, skill, expert, goal, increase, team).
  They are kept in both courses because each course has its own Word Bank.
- Order follows the source: page 2 left column, page 2 right column, page 3 left column, page 3 right column.
- Exam context: 50-question CBT, 75 min. Grammar & Vocabulary 20 (10+10), Reading 10, Listening 20 (10+10).
  Vocabulary questions come from Q Skills 1 R/W and L/S units 1–6 **plus this list**.
- The spec links the official Quizlet set: http://tiny.cc/6hptzz
