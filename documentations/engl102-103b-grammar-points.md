# ENGL 102 / 103B — Midterm Grammar Points (and overlap with ENGL114)

Source: *ENGL 102 Midterm Exam Specs* (PDF, "Midterm CBT — ENG 102 (Humanities)"), page 1 ("Grammar") and
page 4 ("Grammar Sample Questions"). Compared against the 13 topics in
[`courses/engl114/grammar/reference.json`](../courses/engl114/grammar/reference.json)
(see also [engl114_grammar_a1a2_bilingual.md](engl114_grammar_a1a2_bilingual.md)).

## 1. What the spec says about grammar

- The exam has **50 MCQs**; **10** are grammar (the other 10 in the "Grammar & Vocabulary" block are vocabulary).
- Grammar questions are based on **all the grammar points from the units mentioned** (**Q Skills 1 R/W, units 1–6**)
  **plus** the 12 points listed below. **The spec does not list the unit grammar itself** — only the extra 12 points.
  Getting the unit 1–6 grammar needs the course book (Q: Skills for Success 1, Reading & Writing).
- Everything else about the exam (vocabulary list, reading, listening) is covered elsewhere:
  [engl102-103b-vocabulary-list.md](engl102-103b-vocabulary-list.md).

## 2. The 12 listed grammar points

Overlap key: **Full** = ENGL114 already has a topic that covers it · **Partial** = related topic exists but does not
cover it fully · **None** = not in ENGL114's 13 topics (new content needed).

| # | Grammar point (as written in the spec) | Overlap with ENGL114 | ENGL114 topic (slug) / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Simple present of verb *be* | **None** | ENGL114 has *be* only with continuous tenses (`aux-be-present-continuous`, `aux-be-past-continuous`), not *be* as the main verb (am/is/are + noun/adjective/place). |
| 2 | Contractions with forms of the verb *be* | **None** | I'm / you're / he's / isn't / aren't… Not covered. |
| 3 | Simple present of other verbs | **Full** | `aux-do-present-past` (simple present part: base verb, third-person -s, do/does). |
| 4 | Simple present affirmative statements | **Partial** | `aux-do-present-past` shows the base form after do/does/don't but does not teach plain affirmative statements (I work / she works, spelling of -s/-es). |
| 5 | Simple present with Wh-questions and answers (*be*, other verbs) | **Partial** | `aux-do-present-past` covers do/does questions (yes/no and Wh with other verbs). Wh-questions with *be* (Where is…? Who are…?) and the short answers are not covered. |
| 6 | Simple present affirmative and negative statements | **Full** | `aux-do-present-past` (don't / doesn't + base verb) together with the affirmative form. |
| 7 | Adjectives | **Full** | `adjectives-use-placement` (adjective + noun; noun + be + adjective). |
| 8 | Adverbs + adjectives: *pretty, really, very, extremely, quite* | **None** | Intensifiers before adjectives (very tall, extremely hot). `adjectives-use-placement` is the closest topic but does not include them. Also note vocabulary overlap: *extremely* is on the ENGL102 vocabulary list. |
| 9 | Using pronouns to replace gerunds | **None** | e.g. "Swimming is fun. **It** is good for you." Not covered. |
| 10 | Subject and object pronouns | **None** | I / me, he / him, they / them… Not covered. |
| 11 | Capitalization and punctuation | **None** | Not covered. (ENGL114's `compound-sentences` touches the comma before and/but/or/so only.) |
| 12 | Prepositions of location | **None** | in / on / at / under / next to / above / below… Not covered. (*above*, *below*, *on*, *off* are also on the ENGL102 vocabulary list.) |

**Count:** Full = 3 (#3, #6, #7) · Partial = 2 (#4, #5) · None = 7 (#1, #2, #8, #9, #10, #11, #12).

## 3. What the 10 sample questions test (page 4)

The sample questions go beyond the 12 listed points — they show the real exam also tests the points below.

| # | Question (gap-fill) | Point tested | Overlap with ENGL114 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | She usually ___ coffee before breakfast. (drinks / drink / drinking / to drink) | Simple present, third-person -s, adverb of frequency | **Full** — `aux-do-present-past` |
| 2 | Where does he ___? (to live / lives / living / live) | Wh-question with does + base verb | **Full** — `aux-do-present-past` |
| 3 | A: ___ students? B: Yes, they are. (They are / Are they / Be they / They be) | Yes/no question with *be* (word order) | **None** — simple present *be* |
| 4 | A: When ___ the homework? B: Today. (are you going to submit …) | Wh-question with *be going to* (future) | **None** — *be going to* is not an ENGL114 topic |
| 5 | It ___ in class on time. (is important to be …) | *It is + adjective + to-infinitive* | **None** |
| 6 | You ___ too much junk food. (shouldn't eat …) | Modal *should / shouldn't* + base verb (advice) | **Full** — `modals-attitude` |
| 7 | He ___ travel to Dubai in the vacation. (is going to …) | *be going to* + base verb (future plans) | **None** — see #4 |
| 8 | Please ___! I'm trying to sleep. (don't shout …) | Negative imperative | **None** |
| 9 | My friend ___ me lunch today. (buyed / bought / buys / was buy) | Simple past, irregular verb | **Full** — `aux-do-present-past` (simple past part) |
| 10 | I ___ he was rude. (didn't know / isn't know / wasn't know / didn't knew) | Simple past negative with *did* + base verb | **Full** — `aux-do-present-past` |

**Extra points revealed by the samples (not in the 12-point list):** *be going to* (future), modals *should / shouldn't*,
negative imperatives, *it is + adjective + to…* , simple past (regular/irregular), yes/no questions with *be*.

## 4. Summary of the overlap with ENGL114

ENGL114's 13 topics, and whether ENGL 102/103B needs them:

| ENGL114 topic (slug) | Needed by ENGL 102/103B? |
|---|---|
| `aux-do-present-past` | **Yes** — simple present, negatives, questions; simple past appears in the samples |
| `adjectives-use-placement` | **Yes** — "Adjectives" |
| `modals-attitude` | **Probably** — *should / shouldn't* appears in the sample questions (not in the 12-point list) |
| `aux-be-present-continuous` | Not listed (but check the unit 1–6 grammar) |
| `compound-sentences` | Not listed (punctuation overlaps slightly with "Capitalization and punctuation") |
| `present-real-conditional` | Not listed |
| `future-real-conditional` | Not listed |
| `aux-be-past-continuous` | Not listed |
| `tag-questions` | Not listed |
| `conjunctions-parallel` | Not listed |
| `aux-have-present-perfect` | Not listed |
| `parallel-structure` | Not listed |
| `quantifiers-count-noncount` | Not listed |

**Bottom line:** only **2–3 of ENGL114's 13 topics** are directly reusable (simple present / simple past with *do*, adjectives, and
probably modals). **7 of the 12 listed ENGL 102/103B points have no ENGL114 equivalent**, so the ENGL 102/103B grammar reference
will mostly be new content.
